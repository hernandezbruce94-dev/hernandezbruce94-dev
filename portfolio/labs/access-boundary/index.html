    Defend it in an interview

    Ask: Why should an operator role not automatically read business documents? What changes if a reviewer is assigned a different document? What would make a denial inconclusive? Where would you enforce the decision in a real service?

    Limits: no session validation, policy distribution, cryptography, concurrent access, provider IAM semantics, persistence, or live enforcement. The expiry model does not simulate revocation of existing sessions. A reviewer may approve their own document in this model; add a separate-person requirement if the business needs segregation of duties.
